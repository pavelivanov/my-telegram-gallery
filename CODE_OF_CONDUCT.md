# Community standards

This project expects respectful, constructive participation in repository,
support, and community spaces. External contributions and public community
channels are paused until the license decision, an enforcement team, and a
private conduct-reporting channel are approved.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, patient, and considerate when reviewing work or asking for help.
- Critique ideas and outcomes without attacking people.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and the boundaries of volunteer
  maintainers.
- Use synthetic data in reproductions and remove credentials and personal data.
- Accept a maintainer's request to stop disruptive or unsafe behavior.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
misgendering, doxxing, impersonation, spam, and publication of private security,
tenant, payment, or credential data are unacceptable.

## Enforcement readiness

Maintainers may hide or remove content and restrict participation to protect the
project and its users. A public launch is blocked until named moderators document
private reporting, conflicts of interest, evidence retention, appeal, and
escalation procedures. No confidential conduct report should be sent to a
public issue. Vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); product and payment
support use [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
